Privacy Policy

PharmaChain OS (minimum viable product) · Last updated 25 November 2026

PharmaChain OS is a demonstration of supply-chain software for the pharmaceutical industry. This page explains what data the app handles. It applies to the website and to the Android app, which displays the same website.

Demo data only

All companies, products, batches, shipments and stock shown in the app are fictional sample data. The app is not intended for real patients, real prescriptions or real commercial transactions. Its drug-interaction alerts come from a small demonstration knowledge base and are not medical advice.

What we collect

  • No accounts. The app has no sign-up or login and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Text you type in. Anything you enter in a form (for example a sample patient name or a test company) is stored on our server as part of the shared demo data, which can be reset at any time. Please do not enter real personal or health information.
  • Server logs. Like most websites, our web server may keep standard technical logs (IP address, time, requested page) for security and troubleshooting. These are not used to identify or profile you.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, files or location.

Storage on your device

The app may save its own files (the app shell) in your browser so it opens faster and works offline, and it remembers which stockist or pharmacy you last selected for the current session. You can clear this at any time by clearing the site’s data or uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, please open an issue on the project’s source-code repository on GitHub.
